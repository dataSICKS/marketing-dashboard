---
name: Newsletter subject changes
description: A scenario can retain its identity while its delivered email subject changes.
---

## Rule
Treat scenario identity and email subject as independent. A single scenario may contain old and new subjects within the selected reporting period; a representative first subject must not imply that all deliveries used it.

**Why:** The user compares subject changes within the same scenario and reported an old subject still appearing after a change.

**How to apply:** Check the actual subject recorded for each delivery date before assigning a change boundary. Keep multiple subjects and missing subjects visible in grouped views, and distinguish source freshness from display aggregation. A before/after rate difference is not proof of a causal subject-line effect.
